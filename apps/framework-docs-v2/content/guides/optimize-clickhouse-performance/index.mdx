# Optimizing ClickHouse Performance with AI

<Callout type="info" title="TL;DR">
  - This guide is for MooseStack apps that already have a production ClickHouse
  query path that can be faster.
  - The optimization workflow profiles that query, proposes candidate schema
  changes, benchmarks them, and opens a PR for the winning candidate.
  - Your job is to review the benchmark evidence and migration plan, then decide
  whether the winner is ready as-is or needs a safer rollout plan before
  production.
</Callout>

You need the `514` CLI installed, signed in, and your repo linked to your Fiveonefour project (see the **Setup** section in the [tutorial](/guides/optimize-clickhouse-performance/tutorial) and [Hosting](/hosting)). The tutorial then covers **Optimization workflow** and **PR review** in separate sections.
